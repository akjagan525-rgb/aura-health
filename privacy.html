Privacy notice · 12 September 2026

Your question should stay as private as possible.

Please read this before using Aura. By ticking the privacy confirmation on the home page, you agree to this processing for that request.

Do not enter: your name, phone number, email, address, ID number, medical-record number, photos of reports, or another person’s private information.

What is sent to create an answer

When you submit a question, the text of that question and your selected language are sent from Aura’s Netlify function to Google’s Gemini AI service to generate a response. The service also receives the technical information needed to operate the request.

What Aura keeps

Aura does not intentionally save your health question in its own database. Netlify and Google may process logs or data according to their own terms and privacy practices. Do not use Aura for information you would not want processed by those services.

Feedback and contact

If you select a feedback button, Aura sends only the rating, selected language, and page path through Netlify Forms—not your question. If you use the contact form, the name, email, and message you choose to enter are sent through Netlify Forms.

Voice input

Voice input is optional and only starts after you press the microphone button. Your browser may use its own speech-recognition service. Review your browser provider’s privacy information before using voice input.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit a question, use typed input instead of voice, avoid optional feedback, and contact the site owner about a message you sent through the contact form. Aura has no user accounts and does not offer a way to retrieve or delete text that you did not submit through its contact form.

To raise a privacy request, use the contact form with “Privacy request” in the subject. Do not put medical details in that request.
