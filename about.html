About Aura

A calmer way to start a health conversation.

Aura Health is a bilingual English and Tamil education tool. It turns a general health question into plain-language context, low-risk next steps, care guidance, and trusted reading links.

Important: Aura is not a medical device, diagnosis service, or emergency service. It has not been clinically validated or reviewed by a medical panel.

What Aura does

It helps you prepare for a better conversation with a qualified clinician by explaining general health information in an approachable way.

  • English and Tamil interface
  • Plain-language explanations
  • Care and emergency guidance

What Aura does not do

It cannot examine you or know your full medical history. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, calculate medication doses, or replace professional care.

  • Do not use it for emergencies
  • Do not share identifying details
  • Do not delay care because of an answer

Safety by design

Some emergency warning phrases are screened before the AI service is called. Responses are asked to be cautious and are paired with selected NHS and WHO reading links. This is a safety aid, not a guarantee that every urgent situation will be detected.

Professional readiness

Before presenting Aura as a clinical product, arrange independent clinical review, user testing, accessibility testing, and legal/privacy review for the places where it will be used. When you own a domain, connect it in Netlify’s Domain management settings and update your contact details on this site.

For severe, sudden, or worsening symptoms, contact local emergency services or a qualified clinician immediately.
