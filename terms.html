Using Aura means you understand the limits below. These terms should be reviewed by a qualified legal professional before a wider public launch.

Emergency: do not use Aura for severe chest symptoms, trouble breathing, stroke-like symptoms, major bleeding, serious allergic reactions, or a risk of self-harm. In India, call 112 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Education only

Aura provides general health education. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, treatment plan, prescription, or a substitute for a qualified clinician who can assess your personal situation.

Your responsibility

Use your judgment and seek prompt in-person care for severe, new, persistent, worsening, or worrying symptoms. Never delay emergency care because of anything shown by Aura.

AI-generated content can be incomplete, inaccurate, or unsuitable for your context. The health links are selected reading resources, not proof that a particular condition applies to you.

Safe use
